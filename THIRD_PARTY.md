The game builds on [Mr.doob’s Slop New York](https://github.com/mrdoob/slop-newyork), including procedural vehicles and pedestrians, facade and street shading, trees, camera conventions, post-processing and portions of sound design. The original MIT notice is preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Calgary geography, landmarks and gameplay adaptations do not change third-party licenses.

The Bow plaza’s wire portrait is an authored representation of [Wonderland by Jaume Plensa](https://jaumeplensa.com/works-and-projects/public-space/wonderland-2012). Calgary Transit’s red-and-white bus and heritage CTrain appearances are modeled from [City of Calgary fleet references](https://www.calgary.ca/green-line/about/calgary-transit-network.html) and [Calgary Transit vehicle photography](https://www.calgarytransit.com/content/dam/transit/plans---projects/Calgary%20Transit%20-%20Efficiency%20in%20Motion.pdf).

The façade reference photographs retain their individual **CC BY** or **CC BY-SA** licenses. Creators, original sources, capture dates, license links and checksums are recorded in [public/imagery/CREDITS.md](public/imagery/CREDITS.md) and `data/imagery.json`. The game displays their credits in Façade references. The photographs are retained as reference files and the runtime uses authored materials, lettering and geometry.

The distant terrain profile is derived from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, using SRTM and GMTED2010 data courtesy of the **U.S. Geological Survey**. Elevation samples cover 15–180 km from central Calgary. The western sector uses quarter-degree bearings and roughly 135-metre radial sampling. Other bearings retain the original one-degree profile. A 400-metre elevation grid blends the foothills into the outer edge of the playable city. Ground colours, snow, and haze are approximations. The regional terrain is visual only.
